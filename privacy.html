KeyFlips Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

In short: KeyFlips runs entirely in your browser. It does not send your data to us, we do not run any servers that receive it, and we never sell or share it. The only outside services KeyFlips talks to are Gumroad (to check your license), Keepa (with your own API key, if you add one), and Amazon Seller Central (with your own login, for the gating check).

What KeyFlips is

KeyFlips is a Chrome extension that adds estimated profit, ROI, maximum buy price, sales and listing-eligibility (gating) information to the Keepa Deals page at keepa.com.

KeyFlips does not collect your name, address, payment details, browsing history, location, keystrokes, or the contents of any page other than the Keepa Deals table.

Amazon Seller Central

If the gating check is turned on, KeyFlips asks Seller Central whether your seller account can sell each profitable product. It does this from your own browser using the Seller Central login you are already signed in with. KeyFlips never sees, stores or transmits your Amazon password, and it does not read any other part of your Seller Central account.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Gumroad. KeyFlips never receives or stores your card or payment information. Gumroad's handling of your purchase is covered by Gumroad's privacy policy.

Third-party services

  • Gumroad: license verification. Privacy policy
  • Keepa: product data, only with your own API key. Privacy policy
  • Amazon Seller Central: gating check, only with your own login. Privacy notice

KeyFlips is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Keepa or Amazon.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies of our own.
  • We don't use your data for anything other than KeyFlips' single purpose, and never to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We don't load or run code from outside the extension.

Keeping or deleting your data

Everything KeyFlips stores lives in your own browser. You can delete it at any time by clicking Remove key in the KeyFlips popup, clearing the extension's data, or uninstalling KeyFlips, which removes all of it.

Children

KeyFlips is a business tool for Amazon sellers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or KeyFlips can be sent through the contact option on the KeyFlips Gumroad page.
